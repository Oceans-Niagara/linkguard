Field Guide (what the physics metrics mean)
  • Shannon Ceiling C = B log₂(1 + SNR) — theoretical information-theory limit of the channel before MAC framing or duty-cycle caps.
  • Protocol Goodput — realistic payload throughput after LoRa airtime/duty-cycle, BLE ATT intervals, Wi-Fi contention, or UART framing.
  • MTU Fragments — exceeding link MTU splits each message into multiple frames, multiplying header overhead and compound BER loss.
  • BER Loss Prob — 1 - (1 - BER)^(wire_bits); longer or fragmented frames fail exponentially more often at low SNR.
  • Max Sustainable Hz — highest steady-state rate before offered wire bps exceeds 100% of protocol goodput.
